How a project runs

From site visit to handover in four steps

  1. Step 1

    Site visit & measurement

    An engineer visits your site, takes measurements and listens to what you want done.

  2. Step 2

    Itemised written estimate

    Quantities, rates and the brand for each material, written down before you commit.

  3. Step 3

    Work under supervision

    Civil, electrical and interior crews are scheduled in the right order with a supervisor on site.

  4. Step 4

    Handover & snag check

    We walk through the finished work with you and fix any snags before we close the job.

Questions

Before you call

Not answered here? Call or message us on WhatsApp.

Do you take turnkey contracts?+

Yes. We take a project from civil work and wiring through to furniture, ceilings and painting under one contract, one schedule and one estimate. You can also hire us for a single trade.

What paint, wire and cement brands do you use?+

We use standard, established brands for wires, paints, cement and hardware. The brand and grade for every item are written in your estimate, and you can ask for a different brand before work begins.

Do you provide site visits in Bhopal?+

Yes. Yes. Our team visits sites across the whole Bhopal region, including Mandideep, Sehore and Bairagarh. For a site outside Bhopal, call or message us and we will confirm if we can take it up.

How do you price a job?+

After measuring the site, we send an itemised estimate with quantities, rates and materials. Anything outside that scope is discussed and agreed with you before we do it.

Do you work on shops, offices and clinics?+

Yes. We handle commercial fit-outs and renovations as well as homes and villas, including electrical load planning, ceilings, partitions and woodwork.

How long will my project take?+

It depends on the scope. After the site visit we give you a written timeline together with the estimate, so you know the start and handover dates before you commit.
